Baduk Cast

Shows the Awesome Baduk board (and the teacher, if you like) on your TV. Problems stay on your phone.

One-time setup (Android Chrome)

  1. Copy this code:
  2. In Chrome, bookmark any page (☆), then open Bookmarks, edit the new bookmark: name it baduk and replace the URL with the copied code. Save.

Each lesson

  1. Open the lesson on awesomebaduk.com (logged in, as usual).
  2. Tap the address bar, type baduk and tap the bookmark with the star in the suggestions. A small bar appears at the bottom right.
  3. Tap 📺 Cast and choose your TV. The phone video goes quiet; sound plays on the TV.
  4. 🙂 Face switches the teacher video on the TV on or off.

Keep the phone screen on during the video: the TV follows the phone's video position.
